About the project

A fashion photoshoot developed with artificial intelligence as an original portfolio project. The goal was to produce images with the visual quality of an editorial production, working on composition, light direction, textures and atmosphere to build a cohesive aesthetic narrative.

The process combined AI image generation with stages of selection, retouching and refinement, seeking a balance between the technical rigor of a fashion production and the creative freedom that technology allows. Each image was designed within the same visual language, with a palette, contrast and framing that speak to each other.
