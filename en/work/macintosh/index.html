About the project

This video tells the story of the Macintosh: how Apple, pressured by the sales failures of the Apple III and the Lisa, bet on a graphical interface accessible to everyone, culminating in the legendary “1984” commercial, directed by Ridley Scott, which cemented its place in tech history.

The challenge was to turn a technical subject into a clear and engaging story. I worked on the editing pace, the motion design and the visual elements to guide the viewer from start to finish, keeping their attention at every turn of the story.

The result is a dynamic video with its own identity, showing in practice how script, editing and motion design work together to tell a good story.

Project developed in Photoshop and After Effects.
