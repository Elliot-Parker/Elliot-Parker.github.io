Who I am

From script to final delivery, all in one place.

I work as a video producer, motion designer and designer, taking care of the project from start to finish: script, filming, animation, visual identity and delivery. Fewer vendors along the way means less noise, less rework and faster turnaround.

I also use AI applied to images and video to work faster and create distinctive visuals, while staying true to the brand identity.
